📈 Progressive Overload
⬆️

Add Weight

+2.5–5 kg on compounds every 1–2 weeks, only when the top of the rep range is clean

🎯

Rep Target

Hit top of rep range on every set → increase weight next session

📓

Track Everything

Logbook or app — non-negotiable. No numbers, no progression

😴

Sleep

7–9 hours, and Monday is not a training day — it is a recovery day

⚙️ Why Monday Off, Midweek On
🔁

72-Hour Spacing

Every muscle is trained twice with ≥3 days between hits: chest Tue/Fri, back Wed/Sat, legs Thu/Sun

🪫

Deload Every 5th Week

Cut sets by 40%, load to ~70%, keep the movements. Six sessions a week needs a scheduled exit ramp

📉

Autoregulate

Resting HR +7 bpm, or under 6 h sleep → drop the top set and take 20% off the bar

🚫

No Grinding

Never take squats, RDLs or hip thrusts to failure. Keep 1–2 reps in reserve everywhere

⏱️

Cap the Session

75 min hard ceiling. Past that you are adding fatigue, not stimulus

🩹

Don't Stack Mondays

Missed a session? Skip it, don't train Tue and Wed back to back — that breaks the 72 h spacing that makes this split work
